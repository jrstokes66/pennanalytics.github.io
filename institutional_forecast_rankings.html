Key Findings

Horizon:
The forecast look-ahead window. Near-term covers forecasts 1–12 months ahead; Intermediate covers 13–24 months; Long-term covers 25+ months. Variables for which the WSJ survey does not solicit longer-horizon forecasts show fewer tabs.

Min N:
The minimum number of matched forecast–actual pairs required for an institution to qualify. Set to the highest threshold that still yields at least 10 qualifying institutions among those active in the July 2026 survey. Higher N means greater confidence that rankings reflect consistent skill rather than a short lucky streak.

±1 SE:
One standard error around each institution’s RMSE estimate. Where error bars overlap, the rank difference is not statistically reliable and the institutions should be considered statistically indistinguishable.

RMSE:
Root Mean Squared Error — the square root of the average squared forecast error. Lower is better. For rate and inflation variables, units are percentage points (pp); for Payrolls, units are thousands of jobs (k jobs).
