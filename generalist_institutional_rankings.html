Key Findings

Min Vars:
The minimum number of economic variables for which an institution must have a qualifying RMSE to appear in the rankings. Qualifying requires N ≥ the specialist minimum-N threshold for that variable. Higher min vars = stricter breadth requirement. At 9 (all variables), the generalist ranking is directly comparable to specialist results.

Avg Percentile Rank:
Mean of per-variable percentile ranks across all qualifying variables (higher = better). Percentile rank is immune to cross-variable scale differences: a 90th-percentile CPI forecast and a 90th-percentile Payrolls forecast contribute equally, regardless of raw RMSE magnitudes.

Cell colors:
Each variable cell shows an institution’s percentile rank within the full active pool for that variable. Grey cells indicate the institution has fewer observations than the specialist minimum-N threshold and does not qualify for that variable slot.

Min N:
Per-variable minimum observation counts (specialist thresholds): 10Y Tsry ≥60 · CPI YoY ≥128 · Core PCE ≥19 · Fed Funds ≥60 · GDP 4Q4Q ≥19 · GDP QoQ ≥196 · PCE ≥22 · Payrolls ≥7 · Unemp ≥132.
